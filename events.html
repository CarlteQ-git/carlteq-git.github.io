FAQ

Common Questions

What size of events do you cater for?

We currently focus on small and medium-sized events to ensure a high-quality, well-managed experience.

Can I book a full package or rent individual items?

Both. You can choose a curated package (birthday, wedding, or custom) or rent specific items like tents, chairs, and tables on their own.

Do you handle delivery, setup, and takedown?

Yes. For rentals and packages, we deliver, set everything up, and come back after the event to take it down — all-inclusive service.

Can you help us find or book a venue?

Yes. We can help with booking or coordinating venues such as hotels and gardens. If you already have a venue, we’ll plan around your space.

How do I get a quote or recommendation?

Share your event type, date, location, and estimated guest count. We’ll consult with you and recommend the right setup. (We don’t list fixed prices — every event is tailored.)
